## 🧠 THE IDEA

Techfest is more than an annual festival.

It is a meeting point for **technology, people, ideas, competition and innovation**.

This concept reimagines the Techfest homepage around its **30th edition**, creating a digital experience that feels:

> **Bold. Young. Intelligent. Energetic.**

The central message:

### TECHNOLOGY DOESN'T WAIT.

### NEITHER DO WE.

---

## ✦ EXPERIENCE

The landing page is designed as a modern technology-event experience rather than a conventional college-fest website.

### The experience focuses on:

* ⚡ Strong visual storytelling
* 🧠 Clear information hierarchy
* 🎯 Event discovery
* 🖱️ Interactive UI
* ✨ Motion and micro-interactions
* 📱 Responsive design
* 🚀 Gen-Z visual language

---

## 🎨 DESIGN DIRECTION

### CLEAN × BOLD × TECH

The visual system combines:

`Editorial Typography`
`Technology`
`Photography`
`Motion`
`IIT Bombay Identity`

The goal was to create something that could realistically evolve into the next generation of **Techfest.org**.
